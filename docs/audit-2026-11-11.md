# Whole-system audit, 11 Nov 2026

The owner asked for every component to be gone through: every bug, every thing that costs speed, everything that
should happen and does not. This is what was read, what was found, what was fixed and how each fix was checked, and
what is still open. Nothing here claims the system is perfect: the open list at the end is real.

**How it was done.** Every service's log on the server (recorder, copy job, qualify, discover, measure, book, events,
commands, strategist, operator, ops), the database's own tables (queue, verdicts, coverage), the agents' usage lines
(`operator/spend.jsonl`) and turn records (`operator/turns.jsonl`), and the code each finding pointed at. Every fix
has a test that failed first; the full suite was green before each of the three deploys (02:30, 02:50, 03:02 UTC).

## Checked and found right

- **Our tape's order inside a block is exact.** In 20 minutes of 9 Nov, 9,440 of 9,440 same-block neighbours and
  23,152 of 23,152 cross-block neighbours continue the coin's reserves. Every "N trades behind" result rests on this.
- **The agents' price table** matches the pricing page (11 Nov).
- **Measure, book, events, commands, strategist**: no errors in their logs. The three running snipers were not touched.

## The crew rule on our own tape (after fix 3)

A buy of 0.2 SOL where a hunt buy really lands (three trades behind, a block after the trigger), at the measured cost
of 0.00421 SOL a transaction, 137 hours of tape. "Newer" is the part of the launches the exit was not chosen on.

| Rule (a crew wallet among the buyers, and in the first 1.5 s at least…) | Launches | Exit | Newer launches | Mean a trade | Lower bound | Wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 SOL bought | 27 | +30 % / -20 % / 30 s | 11 | +0.048 SOL (+24 %) | +0.045 | 11 of 11 |
| 15 SOL bought | 32 | 30 s | 13 | +0.029 SOL (+15 %) | +0.009 | 85 % |
| 5 SOL bought | 55 | 30 s | 22 | +0.063 SOL (+32 %) | +0.035 | 82 % |

This agrees with the bought month (median +21 % a minute later). The limits: small samples, all from 6-8 Nov; the crew
has not bundled a launch on our tape since 8 Nov 12:20 (its wallets traded 8 and 3 coins on 9 and 10 Nov, none in a
create block); OKTech's wallet-list gate has never run live with our own list.

## Still open

1. **Holes in the tape on every restart and every stream drop**: 56 holes, 121 minutes in the last 2.2 days (two of
   32-35 minutes); 4 % of all tape time since 5 Nov. The feed can replay from a block number (about a day back); the
   PumpSwap side already does, the main stream does not. Next build.
2. **The database grows about 7 GB a day and a copy needs that much room again while it is written.** At 30 days of
   retention the two no longer fit the disk (644 GB) in late November: the copy job will then skip copies (it checks
   room first) and labs fall back to the live file. Fix before then: shorter retention, or one file per day.
3. **The server's memory limit for our services is hit constantly** (680,000 events). The copy every three hours
   instead of every hour takes away most of the cause; raising the limit itself needs the owner's root step.
4. **Whether qualify's lab runs on six processes or one** is not known yet: the next run's output says (`lab.workers`).
5. **What the agents cost is mostly the model and the cadence**: the fixes above take off the repeated part. Half the
   cadence halves the rest. That choice is the owner's.
6. **Failed duplicate sell transactions** (idea I7) cost on every live sell; changing that is a live setting.
