## Lessons

- One bundle operator is behind almost every positive hunt and copy result of rounds 2-3. Its coins were 12 of 15 creator-0 firings on 7 Nov 08-14, all 24 early buys of 7aPo3n in 5 windows, and 7 of 7 creator-0 j7tracker firings. Its marks: 41-44 SOL from 15-21 wallets in the first slot, creator 0.
- Check what a lab's test part holds: both positive labs this round (+0.0355 on 9 trades, +0.0231 on 10) put it on the operator's rug-free hours, 7 Nov 12:32-19:22.
- Unused old tape settles a lead faster than forward firings: 20.8 h of 4-5 Nov tape refuted the creator-0 hunt about a day early (19 firings, gross -0.5 %, -0.0099 a trade).
- A bundle operator's coins need a short exit. They rose a median +35 % in 20 s, but 5 of 25 rugged within 20 s and 11 of 25 were down 50 %+ at 60 s. I6 with a 60 s stop-and-trail exit made -0.0553 a trade on 8 unseen firings.
- At take profit +30 % a winner nets about +0.05 and a one-trade rug about -0.175 at 0.2 SOL. Such a hunt breaks even at about one rug in five coins.
- A copy lands about 7 slots after a hunt on the same coins and gives up about +0.0115 a trade: with a 20 s timer, +0.0022 against +0.0137 (n 25).
- The operator works in bursts: 3 sessions of 1-2.5 h (10-12 coins each) in 28 scanned hours, with none in 6 Nov 16:15-22:15 or 7 Nov 04:15-08:15. Forward counts arrive in bursts.
- Launch tools do not pick better coins: 0 of 184 host x rule x exit cells passed on two windows. The probe's best small hosts lost -0.005 to -0.036 a trade.
- A uri deny list is hygiene, not an edge. Deny hosts plus Qm links cause 81 % of the 2 SOL / 1 s hunt's failed buys, but each skip saves only 0.003-0.004 SOL plus the sniper's shot.
- Many wallets buying little does not happen in a coin's first second: only 3 of 47 breadth-hunt firings had under 5 SOL bought, and its lab lost -0.0028 a trade (n 76).
- Calm hours do not rescue crowd hunts. After 18:00 the declared TP+30/SL-20/30 s made +0.31 % gross on 103 calm fills (10/6/1), against +4.5 % needed. Round 2's +10 % was one 2-hour stretch.
- Firing-rate gates do not work on 7 Nov tape. The 10/10/1 rule fired 20-54 times an hour all evening, and the firings a 40-an-hour gate would skip did better (+10-12 % on 19 fills).
- Riding hunt coins to their migration loses out of sample: -13 % gross with a stop, -23 % without (152 fills). That holds although riders (4.6-8.2 % of fills) reach 3.6-9x.
- The lab drops firings whose coin migrates within 300 s wherever the PumpSwap tape is missing, and for short exits those are winners. Lab 63076f skipped 5 of the operator's 8 coins that completed within 300 s.
- A close landing is still not an edge: latecrowd copies landed at +0.2 % median and lost -0.0155 to -0.0191 a copy (1,906 copies). The late crowd is the coin's own traffic: buyers after the leader match buyers before it (ratio 1.02-1.08).
