## Lessons

- Creator-0 pump.fun hunts fire about once an hour or less, so a 48 h lab's test holds 9-15 trades. The 100-trade freeze bar cannot be met; only forward counts decide them.
- Pump.fun-only creator-0 labs are positive (+0.0406 on 12, +0.0347 on 14, +0.0187 on 11), but about 75-90 % of the test rows are the 41 SOL operator's coins.
- Pre-knob labs scored LaunchLab about 0.23 SOL too kindly over 11 trades (job d39640: +0.0214 reported, +0.0015 replayed). Discard every creator-0 lab from before b52c4ca.
- Mayhem coins never pass a crowd gate (0 of 1,803 reached 1 SOL from 3 buyers). Holder-reward coins are the crowd-gate polluter, at 23-38 % of the coins that pass.
- Taking out mayhem and holder-reward coins does not rescue crowd hunts: the burst plain 10/10 lab lost -0.0150 on 156 against the screen's +15 % gross at a fixed 30 s.
- A fixed-exit SQL screen can be badly kinder than the lab: +15.4 % gross in the screen became -0.015 net in the lab test. Always lab a screen before believing it.
- Train mean is an upper bound again: 10/10/1 dev0 train +0.088, test +0.019 (-80 %); burst plain train +0.019, test -0.015.
- Real neighbours on operator mode A coins: sold within 20 s +18.1 % gross, held longer -20.8 %. The gap between real money and the simulator is mostly about exit timing.
- The lab's MIG scoring agrees with SQL: -0.0119 on 66 in the lab against the SQL screen's -0.010 to -0.014.
- Consensus among fast bots adds nothing: 2-of-20 made +0.9 % gross on 294, no better than a single list buy.
- When ranking fast wallets, count only buys before our entry slot. Later buys lift the 30 s mark mechanically (look-ahead).
- TP/SL with the 1.3 s watch delay destroys short-hold edges on coins that can dump in one trade (picker +8.9 % to -1.7 %). Test pure timers separately.
- token_meta has no metadata after 5 Nov 17:29, so every metadata or uri angle is blind forward.
- The clock does not pay: the best UTC block flips day to day and the spread between sessions is about 0.003, against about 0.009 of cost. Rugs cluster at 13-22 UTC (19 % against 4 %).
- The 41 SOL operator keeps no clock and dumps within its first 10 minutes. Activity arming would arm too late; stay armed all the time instead.
- Round 2's MIG-10 Qm pre-registration is refuted: -0.0137 at 0.5 on 39, the coins outside the top 5 sum to -0.849.
