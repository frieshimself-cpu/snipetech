YOUR "LIVE TRADING NEVER STOPS"
- It is built and deployed (d682e4d). The guard's stops can be switched off. The automatic test has no block limit, and when one strategy is dropped it moves to the next one that passes.
- It only trades a strategy that passes the forward paper test at our real landing: 100 or more trades and a lower bound above zero. None passes, so it waits. That wait is the "be smart about it" from your 13:30 message. It is not a fault.

HOW IT WAS TESTED
- Seven angles (wide hunts, timing, exits, quiet copy leaders, creators, migration, contrarian) produced 24 hypotheses.
- About 70 read-only SQL screens on the 5-7 Nov tape each checked a prediction.
- 8 ideas could be written as lab triggers. Each was replayed where live buys land:
  - Launch rules buy one slot after the trigger, 3 trades in ("+3@1").
  - Copies buy up to 6 trades behind the leader ("+6").
- The exit is picked on the oldest 60 % of moments. The newest 40 % is the test.

LAB RESULTS (test part, SOL per 0.2 SOL trade, as the lab reported them; "creator" = the coin creator's own first buy)
1. Hunt, 10+ SOL from 10+ wallets within 1 s, creator 1 SOL or less: 234 trades, -0.0003, lower bound -0.0041. The training part was -0.0060. This is the least bad.
2. Hunt, 1+ SOL within 2 s, creator bought nothing: 147 trades, -0.0009, lower bound -0.0066. Training part +0.0025. Only 24 % of trades won, so a few runners carry the result.
3. Every normal launch, skipping mayhem and holder-reward coins: 2,877 trades, -0.0035, lower bound -0.0051. It was scored at a better landing than OKTech gets, and OKTech cannot skip mayhem coins anyway.
4. A normal coin past 85 % of its curve, bought at the copy landing: 129 trades, -0.0041. OKTech cannot buy mid-curve.
5. Hunt, 40+ SOL within 1 s: 55 trades, -0.0094. The training part made +0.0264, then flipped.
6. Hunt, 20+ SOL from 40+ wallets within 30 s, creator 1 SOL or less: 227 trades, -0.0106.
7. Hunt, 10+ SOL within 3 s from at most 3 wallets, creator 1 SOL or less: 122 trades, -0.0107. The lab chose a "sell after 1 quiet second" exit, not the 10-15 s timer the idea depended on.
8. Hunt, 20+ SOL from 20+ wallets within 10 s: 721 trades, -0.0145.

CORRECTIONS TO THOSE NUMBERS
- Cost: every lab charged the lab's default 0.0035 SOL a transaction, which is 0.007 a round trip. Live costs about 0.009.
  - I read six of the job records; none set a different cost, and the research workflow's lab step never sets one.
  - So every result above is 0.002 SOL a trade too kind, or 0.003 for exits that sell in two parts.
- Watch delay: the lab's hunt exits ignore the 1.3 s before OKTech starts watching a coin. That costs another 0 to 0.003 a trade.
- In live conditions the best result (#1) is about -0.002 to -0.005 a trade.
- #1 and #2 make about +0.006 a trade after pump.fun's fees and our own price impact. That does not cover the 0.009 fixed cost of a round trip.
- The lab-stage note on #8 says its trigger "cannot be known a slot after the create". That is wrong. The lab buys a slot after the trade that met the trigger, which is what OKTech does. The -0.0145 stands.

WHY THE OTHER IDEAS DIED (SQL screens)
- At the hunt landing the price falls after our buy. Across about 30 settings and 4 time windows, it averaged 0.82-0.98 of our price at 30 s and 0.73-0.98 at 60 s. A trade needs about +8 % to break even.
- No exit fixes that:
  - None of the 6,072 exits OKTech can run comes out positive.
  - A seller who knew the future would make only +0.0037 to +0.0124 a trade by selling within 5 s.
  - 12-13 % of buys are already down more than 10 % before OKTech starts watching.
- Bigger crowds buy the top (#6 and #8).
- Every creator rule loses: no creator buy, big creator buys, first-time creators, and lists of past winning creators. The best fifth of creators went from +0.0171 to -0.0047 in the next half (413 trades).
- Timing does not carry over:
  - None of 48 hourly hunt results is positive.
  - One hour's result predicts the next hour's only weakly (correlation 0.235).
  - Hours flooded with launches are the worst (-0.067 at 60 s).
- Quiet copy leaders lose more than crowded ones. Wallets with no followers lose -0.0154 a trade (17 wallets).
  - Quiet wallets that won before fade to +2.8 to +3.4 % before costs. A copy needs +8 %.
  - The Raydium LaunchLab "winners" were one operator's 36-minute spree on 6 Nov, silent since.
- Also dead:
  - buying the dip after a frenzy: -0.005 to -0.041;
  - coins priced in something other than SOL: -0.026 to -0.031;
  - copying pump.fun's mayhem agent (the bot that trades mayhem-mode coins): -0.018 to -0.043;
  - coins reviving after a crash: -0.15;
  - MIG windows wider than 10 s: they buy mostly mayhem coins, and 17 of 18 lost more than 80 % within 15 minutes.

WHAT IS STILL OPEN, RANKED
1. MIG with its default 10 s window (the time from a coin's create to its migration).
   - It only buys coins whose launch bundle fills the whole curve at once: about 260 a day, none of them mayhem.
   - PumpSwap opens at 2.9 times the curve's top price. The price then drifts up +4.3 % at 30 s and +6.6 % at 300 s (20 coins). A round trip costs about 6.8 %.
   - That is break-even at best: holding 300 s gave -0.0007 a trade, give or take 0.017.
   - To score it: deploy the mig-trigger and migration-sell branches, and wait for 24 h of PumpSwap tape (about 8 Nov 14:00 UTC; the 14:03-15:48 tape stall cost 1 h 45 min).
   - To trade it unattended: the automatic test must accept MIG (it accepts hunts only), and /sell must be able to sell PumpSwap coins (it sells curve coins only).
2. Copying nya666, a sniper bot that was retired only for being a "bundle member".
   - Its verdict: 119 trades, +0.0075, lower bound +0.0005. Only 2 % of its buys sit in launch bundles.
   - Against it: 14 other buyers pile in within 2 s of its buys, like the leaders whose live copies all lost. Wallets of its type average -0.0096 (114 wallets). And its lower bound is smaller than the gap between paper and live costs.
   - Worth a 30-copy paper test, nothing more.
3. Buying when a creator collects its fees, on coins 10-60 minutes old that are still trading: +0.024 a trade (48 trades).
   - It is one result out of about 20 cut from the same data, so it is probably luck.
   - A cheap SQL recheck on another window should come before we build a claim recorder.

Everything else needs something built first and has a low chance of paying: counting buyers routed through trading apps, or launches tied to fresh tweets.

WHAT THIS MEANS
The money we have seen sits where OKTech cannot land: inside the create slot. The rest is a few runners that no rule can pick early. MIG is the one place found where landing a little late costs little. A few trades after the migration, the price was within 1 % of the first PumpSwap trade.

## Lessons

- Lab cost: the 7 Nov edge-hunt labs charged the lab's default 0.0035 SOL a transaction, 0.007 a round trip (six job records read). Live is about 0.009, so subtract 0.002 a trade (0.003 with a partial take profit), or pass --per-tx 0.0045.
- Lab watch delay: the grid's hunt exits use watch_secs 0, but OKTech starts watching at 1.3 s. A 2 s-delay replay costs hunt buys 0 to 0.003 a trade.
- No launch rule pays at +3@1: 0 of 8 labs positive on 7 Nov. The least bad (hunt 10 SOL from 10 wallets in 1 s, creator 1 SOL or less) tested -0.0003 (n 234, train -0.006); at live cost and with the watch delay that is about -0.002 to -0.005.
- The two least-bad hunts make about +0.006 a 0.2 SOL trade after pump.fun's fees and our own price impact (test part), but not enough to cover the 0.009 fixed cost of a round trip.
- Creator bought nothing (hunt 1 SOL in 2 s, dev_max 0): -0.0009 a trade (n 147, 24 % wins, train +0.0025). OKTech's reading of maxDevBuySol 0 is unverified.
- Later and bigger crowds buy the top: 20 SOL from 20 wallets in 10 s lost -0.0145 (n 721); 20 SOL from 40 wallets in 30 s lost -0.0106 (n 227).
- Small wins in one window are noise: 10-15 s timer results of +0.017 to +0.034 (n 12-13) reversed at n 99-116 (-0.040 to -0.062). The 40 SOL in 1 s hunt trained +0.026 and tested -0.0094 (n 55).
- Skipping mayhem and holder-reward coins cuts the every-launch loss from about -0.010 to -0.0035 a trade (n 2,877, start@1), but it still loses, and OKTech cannot skip those coins.
- No exit fixes hunt buys: 0 of 6,072 OKTech exits are positive. A seller who knew the future would make only +0.0037 to +0.0124 by selling within 5 s. 12-13 % of buys are down more than 10 % before OKTech starts watching at 1.3 s.
- Timing does not carry over: 0 of 48 hourly hunt results are positive. One hour predicts the next at a correlation of 0.235, and the same hour the next day at 0.18-0.20.
- Flood hours (30 or more hunt triggers in 10 minutes) are the worst: -0.067 at 60 s against -0.0345 for the day. Skipping them can only cut losses.
- Quieter copy leaders lose more at +6: wallets with 0 followers lose -0.0154 a trade (17 wallets), wallets with over 12 make +0.0004 (17). None of the 141 wallets with 2 or fewer followers has a positive lower bound.
- Crowded leaders look least bad in the lab at +6, but their live copies landed at +18-43 % and lost (2YDn 10 of 10, 4yo9 6 of 6). The reason: when the leader is the last trade in its slot, the lab copies at the leader's own price.
- Quiet wallets that won before fade: rankings carry over between windows (0.38-0.51), but on new tape they make +2.8 to +3.4 % before costs, against the +8 % a 0.2 SOL copy needs.
- Lists of past winning creators fail on new data: the best fifth (+0.0171 a trade) made -0.0047 in the next half (n 413).
- Buying near graduation fails: 66 % of coins that cross 85 % of their curve never graduate (0.52 of entry price after 5 minutes). -0.0041 a trade at +6 (n 129).
- MIG-10 buys only coins whose launch bundle fills the curve at once (about 260 a day, none mayhem). PumpSwap opens at 2.88 times the curve top, then drifts +4.3 % at 30 s and +6.6 % at 300 s (n 20), against a round trip of about 6.8 %.
- Never widen MIG past 10 s: wider windows buy mostly mayhem coins, and 17 of 18 fell more than 80 % within 15 minutes.
- Buying when a creator claims its fees on a coin still trading loses about -0.010 a trade (n 135). The +0.024 result for 10-60 minute-old coins (n 48) is one of about 20 cuts of the same data.
- Groups of wallets funded by the same source cannot be screened: the funders table holds 2 rows.
- A slot is 0.2145 s, so 170 slots is 36 s, not 60 s.
- A lab '1 s' hunt window covers 1-2 s of real time, because tape timestamps are whole seconds. OKTech's timeWindowMs 1000 fires on only part of the lab's moments.
- The tape stalled on 7 Nov from 14:03 to 15:48. The PumpSwap tape has that hole, so 24 h of it will exist from about 8 Nov 14:00 UTC.
- Freeze only rules that survive the skeptics. With never-stops and the automatic test, a frozen rule that passes paper trades live by itself, and an 80 % lower bound lets 1 in 5 zero-edge rules clear zero by luck.

## How the lab scores (map stage)

LAB SCORING GUIDE

MOMENTS
- SOL-quoted coins with 5+ trades in a recorded span (--hours: newest N h of the hourly copy).
- Create before the span: launch, hunt, plain, max_age, solo never fire; dev unknown.
- One moment per coin per trigger (first firing), 60 s+ before the span ends; launch kinds (launch, hunt, whale, burst) skip landings off the curve.

FIRING (dev = create signer + creator)
- launch: the create tx's last trade.
- hunt: sums every buy from the create, dev's included; fires at total >= sol with >= buyers wallets (dev counts); skipped past max_buyers wallets, a dev create buy > dev_max, the window's end or a non-SOL quote. OKTech runs it exactly.
- whale: first non-dev buy >= sol; solo refuses create-tx buys and create slots with 3+ non-dev buyers.
- burst: first non-dev buy whose trailing window holds >= buyers wallets and >= sol; any age unless max_age.
- kol: a group wallet's first buy in the coin >= sol (default 0.1).
- momentum: buyers/5 min, sol/30 s, progress, net holders, 5-min return, plus a fixed 2+ buys of 1+ SOL in 60 s.

LANDING AND PRICE
- launch: start@1, first in the next slot (better than hunt fills).
- hunt, whale, burst: +3@1, behind the trigger's slot, then up to 3 trades into the next (5 Nov fills: 0-2 trades later than live), so create-slot bundles are out of reach.
- kol, momentum: +6@0, up to 6 trades behind inside the trigger's slot; a leader last in its slot is copied at its own price (live: +18-43 %).
- Fill: 0.2 SOL, less the recorded fee, on the reserves after the trades ahead.

EXIT GRID (49; no dev-sell, copy exit or OKTech's 1.3 s watch delay)
- dead exit 1/2/5 s x stop 15/30 %, 300 s (6)
- trailing 10/18/25 % x activation 0/+20 % x hold 60/300 s, stop 15 % (12)
- take profit +30/+100 % x hold 30/120 s, stop 20 % (4)
- timers 10/30/60 s, no stop (3)
- scale-out: +10/+20 % selling 35/50 %, rest on stop/trail 8/10, 15/10 or 15/18 %, dead exit off/1 s, 300 s (24)
Sells land after the exit's slot; dead exits sell at the quiet state, even at entry.

TRAIN/TEST, EV_LOWER, FEES
- Oldest 60 % of moments pick the exit by mean; newest 40 % give test n, mean, ev_lower, win_rate, total. Under 20 moments: unranked.
- ev_lower: one-sided 80 % bootstrap lower bound of the test mean; a zero-edge rule clears zero 1 time in 5.
- Fees: recorded on-chain rate each way plus 0.0035 SOL a transaction (0.007 a round trip, 0.0105 with partial take profit). Live is about 0.009: pass --per-tx 0.0045. Failed buys are free.
