## 2. "FOMO" wallets (crowd leaders): already refuted on the tape

OKTech's FOMO feed is a page, not a sniper trigger (docs/oktech-features.md: "Chain / Social / Ultimate / FOMO —
pages and streams, not sniper fields"). The equivalent we can test is a wallet whose buys a crowd follows within
seconds. Round 1 built that screen ("latecrowd": at most 2 other buyers in the leader's slot and the next 2, then 5
or more in the following 10 s) and the record says −0.0155 to −0.0191 per copy on 1,906 copies, even when the copy
lands +0.2 % from the leader (round 6 map, angle 3). The crowd that follows them is what we land behind. Not pursued.

## 3. Dev-wallet snipes: built the lab knob, first screen below

What existed: the `profitable-devs` leaderboard (analysis/deployer.py) scores deployers and gives SNIPE / MARGINAL /
AVOID verdicts, and `oktech.dev_sniper` turns a SNIPE verdict into OKTech's dev sniping (`devWallets`). What was
missing: scoring a chosen deployer's launches in the lab, at the launch landing and the measured cost, with the
lab's exit set. Built today (9ab6b2e, ab50b45):

- `Trigger.creators` on a launch rule: a label kind (`snipetech label --kind dev <address>`) or one deployer's
  address. `{"kind": "launch", "creators": "dev"}` fires only on coins a labelled dev created.
- `oktech strategy` sends a rule limited to one address as `devWallets` on that address (no `monitorProgram`); a
  rule naming the label group is refused, because without addresses it would buy every launch.
- Strategies frozen before keep their ids (creators "" is a later field at its default).

Two warnings before believing any of it. The scorer's sizes are 5 SOL for most rows, so +0.065 a launch is +1.3 %
of the stake; and these are serial launchers (100–330 coins a day each), which is the bundle-operator shape rounds 5
and 6 found to lose at our landing. The lab run (job 20261108-154458-3e628e: pooled "dev" group and one rule per
deployer, 72 h, 0.2 SOL, the lab's exit set, launch landing, measured cost) is the test that counts.

Every one loses or is indistinguishable from zero at the landing a launch snipe gets. The one positive mean
(HWXmR5FU, +0.0032 on 100) wins 18 % of the time with a lower bound of −0.0235: a few spikes carry it, the kind of
row that has turned negative on every forward test so far. The scorer's +0.065 at 5 SOL for the same wallet was
measured at a different landing and size, and does not survive this one. The pooled "dev" rule fired on nothing
because the lab reads the hourly read-only tape copy, which did not yet hold the labels written minutes before (the
per-address rules need no labels); the next hourly copy carries them.

Verdict: dev-wallet sniping of the serial launchers on the leaderboard is refuted at our landing (five deployers,
1,306 out-of-sample trades, all five lower bounds below zero). Nothing is frozen and no sniper is proposed. The knob
stays: any deployer can now be scored the same way in one command, and `profitable-devs` verdicts should be read as
candidates for this lab, not as sizes to arm.

What would change the answer: a deployer whose coins draw outside buyers in slot +1 (the 41 SOL operator's shape,
round 6) rather than a serial launcher; the lab now takes its address directly. The strategist's idea list gets this
as a standing check: when the leaderboard's top row changes, run `lab --trigger {"kind":"launch","creators":"<address>"}`
before believing its verdict.
