# The 6Z91 recipe as rules the lab and the paper test can run (12 Nov, 19:30 UTC)

**The owner, 12 Nov:** "we cant copy a wallet that is copying because we are filled above, we can learn from it tho."

**Answer.** The recipe is built and can be asked for by name: `lab --recipe 6z91` and `paper freeze --recipe 6z91`.
It follows the wallets the bot follows, in the coins it picks, with its size, bid and exit. It never follows the bot.
Checking it against the bot's own trades changed four things in the playbook's one-line recipe, and showed how
little of the bot it covers today:

- **The exit was the wrong one for copies.** The bot does not sell 40 % at 2 seconds on its copies, and it does not
  drop them when the price stands still. On its copies it sells 30 % at 3.2 seconds, then 17 % every 2.7 seconds,
  and is out by 16 to 20 seconds. The 40 % at 2 seconds and the drop belong to its launch snipes and its late buys.
- **The market-cap band was read after the bot's own buy.** A rule can only read the cap after the buy it follows.
  There the same copies stand at 47 to 106 SOL, not 57 to 122.
- **"A buy of 1 SOL" is under 1 SOL on the tape.** A wallet that types 1 SOL puts 0.96 to 0.99 SOL into the coin.
  A rule that asks for 1.0 passes none of them. The recipe asks for 0.95.
- **The recipe fires on the same buy as 29 of the bot's 263 copies of the last 24 hours (11 %).** It fires on 482
  moments; the bot bought in that block on 33 of them (7 %). 112 of the bot's copies follow wallets we do not track.
  The rest is lost to the gates, which are the middle half of what the bot does, not its limits.

A word on what these numbers are. They are the bot's own trades and our wallet lists, read off the tape. Nothing
here is a paper test of ours. Nothing here says the recipe makes money where our buys land: that is what the
commands at the end are for. Nothing was started, tested live or changed on the server.

**Windows.** The bot's trades: 5 Nov 18:04 to 12 Nov 18:04 UTC (7 days; 26,961 trades, 4,902 buys, 4,621 round
trips). The coverage check: 11 Nov 18:04 to 12 Nov 18:04 UTC (24 hours). The playbook this extends:
[the three bots](2026-11-12-winning-bots.md).

## Report

OUTCOME: `--recipe 6z91` expands to five rules and scores the bot's own exit on each. The bot's copy exit is a
clock: 30 % at 3 s, the rest in five pieces every 3 s, out by 18 s; it is in every lab's exit set, and no sniper is
ever sent it. The recipe covers 11 % of the bot's copies in 24 hours, because the bot's wallet list is not ours
(43 % of its copies) and because quartile bands are not its gates. Whether the recipe pays at our landing is not
known: no lab has run it yet (the server does not have this code).

### 1. What was built

| piece | what it does |
|---|---|
| `lab --recipe 6z91` | The recipe's five rules at 2 SOL a buy and a 0.03 SOL bid (`--buy`, `--buy-bid` say another). The result's `recipe.scores` is the bot's own exit on every rule; `strategies` is the lab's best exit on the same moments. |
| `paper freeze --recipe 6z91` | Freezes each rule with the bot's own exit (never the lab's pick of the day), with the bid kept on the rule so the forward run charges it on every buy. `skipped` names each rule left out and why. |
| `min_age` (wallet rules) | The followed buy comes at least this many seconds after the coin's launch. `max_age` was there. |
| `first_part` (exits in parts) | The first part sells its own share; the other parts share the rest equally. |
| `flat_below` (exits in parts) | When the parts are due and the position is worth less than this multiple of its cost, everything goes in one sale. |
| `paper freeze --buy-bid` | A bid on every buy, for any freeze. |

The recipes are one table in `src/snipetech/analysis/lab.py` (`RECIPES`): a name, the rules, the exit, the size, the
bid. A new recipe is one entry.

| recipe | rules | exit | size and bid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `6z91` | A followed wallet's first buy of 0.95 to 3 SOL, the coin 13 s to 7 min old, market cap 47 to 106 SOL after that buy: behind groups `wallet`, `trader`, `big`, `kol`. And the big wallets' first buy of 0.95 SOL or more with no age or cap gate. | 30 % at 3 s, five more parts every 3 s, out by 18 s | 2 SOL, 0.03 |
| `6z91-quick` | the same five rules | 40 % at 2 s, six more parts every 3 s, out by 20 s; everything at once at 2 s when worth under 0.99 of its cost | 2 SOL, 0.03 |
| `6z91-big` | the big wallets' first buy of 0.95 SOL or more | as `6z91` | 10 SOL, 0.3 |
| `fhbh1d-big` | the same rule | everything at once 1 to 2 s after the buy | 5 SOL, 0.35 |

Every exit that sells in parts is research only. `oktech.validate_exit` says so, and the builder of a sniper's
sells (`oktech.autosell`) now refuses one. That second refusal is new and matters: before it, an exit in parts
would have been sent as its plain timer.

One side effect to know. The `6z91` exit is in the set every lab scores, so the lab can pick it as the best exit of
any rule, a launch rule too. Such a rule can be frozen on paper and can never go live, exactly as with momentum
hold: the automatic live test says "cannot be sent to OKTech as it was scored". If that costs the live test a rule it
would otherwise have run, taking the exit out of `exit_set()` is one line; `--recipe` scores it either way.

### 2. What the tape corrected

**The exit.** The playbook's clock (40 % by 2 s, drop at 2 s) is the average over everything the bot does. Split by
what triggered the buy, 7 days, one-buy trades sold on the curve:

| the buy is | trades | SOL made, before bids | sold whole in one sale 1.5 to 3 s after the buy | when it sells in pieces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| in the coin's create block (launch snipe) | 1,671 | +289 | 602 (36 %), −23.7 SOL | 40 % at 1.1 s, 40 % at 4.3 s |
| 1 to 4 blocks after a buy (late) | 815 | +113 | 211 (26 %), −8.5 SOL | 30 % at 3.0 s, 17 % at 5.6 s |
| behind a wallet on our lists, same block | 1,019 | +336 | 1 | 30 % at 3.2 s, then 17 % at 5.8, 9.0 and 11.6 s, 15 % at 13.9 s |
| behind a wallet not on our lists, same block | 670 | +151 | 31 (5 %) | 30 % at 3.0 s, then 17 % at 5.6 and 8.3 s |
| **the copy half (the last two rows)** | **1,689** | **+487** | **32 (2 %)** | **30 % at 3.2 s, 17 % at 5.8, 8.8, 11.3 s, 12 % at 13.7 s, the rest at 16.3 s** |

On its copies the bot has sold 19 % of the coins by 3 s, 32 % by 5 s, 58 % by 10 s, 80 % by 15 s and 94 % by 20 s
(means). The recipe's exit sells 30 % at 3 s and 14 % at 6, 9, 12, 15 and 18 s. The simulator prices a timer on the
tape's whole-second clock, so its "3 s" is 3 to 4 s after the buy.

**The drop at 2 s is a task, not a price rule.** By the size the bot types: on 2.0 and 2.5 SOL copies it dropped
none of 825 whole at 2 s; on 3.0 SOL 15 of 342; on 1.2 and 1.8 SOL (its launch sizes) 26 of 103. Where it does
drop, the price has often not moved: a position nobody traded behind is worth 0.975 of its cost (the fee both ways),
and the median drop is at 0.976 (855 drops, all kinds). But a quarter of the drops are above 1.013 and a quarter
below 0.957. One line on price sorts 58 % of its 1,635 first sells at 1.5 to 3 s the way the bot did; a coin toss
sorts 50 %. So `flat_below` 0.99 (the price is under 1.5 % above our buy) is in `6z91-quick` only, as a rule of our
own to compare, not as the bot's.

**The gates, measured on the copy half (1,954 buys behind another wallet's buy in the same block, 7 days).**

| | 10 % | 25 % | median | 75 % | 90 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| the coin's age at the bot's buy, seconds | 4 | 13 | 58 | 428 | 4,602 |
| the followed buy (the first one ahead of it in the block), SOL into the coin | 0.5 | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.9 | 3.9 |
| market cap after the bot's own buy (1,741 copies on the curve), SOL | 46 | 58 | 81 | 123 | 174 |
| market cap after the followed buy (1,058 curve copies behind our wallets), SOL | 39 | 48 | 68 | 106 | 151 |

- The playbook's "13 seconds to 7 minutes", "57 to 122 SOL" and "1 to 3 SOL" are the 25 % and 75 % marks. They are
  the middle half of what the bot does. Used as gates, three such bands pass about one copy in six even with the
  bot's own wallet list.
- The lab's market-cap gate reads the cap after the followed buy. So the recipe's band is 47 to 106 SOL.
- 213 of the 1,954 copies are on PumpSwap, after the coin left its curve. The band keeps the recipe on the curve.

**A typed 1 SOL.** The tape records the SOL that goes into the coin. pump.fun's 1.25 % comes off what the wallet
types, and a trading terminal takes up to 1 % more. In one hour of tape, buys near 1 SOL were recorded as 0.988
(654 buys), 0.978 (518), 1.000 (58). Of the buys the bot followed, 155 put 0.95 to 1.0 SOL into the coin and 39
put 1.0 to 1.05. Every rule of ours with `"sol": 1` has been passing over typed 1 SOL buys. The recipe uses 0.95.
The analyst's tool now says so.

**First buy or any buy.** In 1,723 of the 1,954 copies (88 %) the first buy ahead of the bot in the block is that
wallet's first buy in the coin. Behind our wallets it is 1,049 of 1,117 (94 %). The lab's rule (a wallet's first buy
in a coin) is the bot's rule nine times in ten. No new knob was added.

**Which of our wallets.** 1,093 of the bot's 1,117 copies behind our wallets are behind names the tracker calls
traders. Group `wallet` also fires behind names like "Faucet" (72 of its 389 moments in 24 hours; the bot took none
of them). So the recipe carries group `trader` next to `wallet`.

### 3. The recipe against the bot, last 24 hours

Definitions, exactly:

- **A copy of the bot:** the first buy of one of its trades, in the same block as and behind another wallet's buy
  of that coin, not in the coin's create block. 263 in the 24 hours (230 on the curve).
- **A moment of a rule:** per coin, the first buy by a wallet of the rule's group that passes every gate (0.95 to
  3 SOL into the coin, the wallet's first buy in that coin, the coin 13 to 420 s old by block time, market cap 47 to
  106 SOL after the buy, not the coin's creator). One moment a coin, as the lab fires. Rebuilt from the tape with
  SQL, not by a lab run.
- **Covered:** a rule's moment is in the same coin and the same block as the bot's copy, ahead of the bot's buy.

| rule | moments in 24 h | wallets followed | the bot bought in that block | the bot bought that coin at another time | the bot never bought that coin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `wallet`, gated | 389 (16 an hour) | 121 | 20 | 108 | 261 |
| `trader`, gated | 333 (14 an hour) | 108 | 23 | 92 | 218 |
| `kol`, gated | 39 | 10 | 5 | 10 | 24 |
| `big`, gated | 3 | 2 | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| `big`, no gates | 34 | 6 | 5 | 14 | 15 |
| **all five, distinct moments** | **482 in 424 coins** | | **33 (7 %)** | | |

**Coverage: 29 of the bot's 263 copies (11 %).** The four gated rules alone: 26 (10 %). `trader` covers 21,
`wallet` 18, `kol` 5, `big` without gates 3.

Why the four gated rules miss the other 237 copies (the first gate that fails):

| copies | why |
|---|---|
| 112 | no wallet of ours is ahead of the bot in the block: it follows a wallet we do not track |
| 50 | the followed buy is under 0.95 or over 3 SOL |
| 41 | the coin is younger than 13 s or older than 7 min |
| 19 | the market cap after the followed buy is outside 47 to 106 SOL |
| 9 | every gate passes, and the rule had already fired on an earlier buy in that coin (one position a coin) |
| 5 | the followed buy is not that wallet's first buy in the coin |
| 1 | the coin's launch is not on the tape |

What the gates cost, on the same day:

| gates | moments of the four rules | copies of the bot covered |
|---|---|---|
| the recipe's (0.95 to 3 SOL, 13 s to 7 min, 47 to 106 SOL) | 450 | 26 (10 %) |
| the 10 % to 90 % marks (0.95 to 3.9 SOL, 4 s to 10 min, 39 to 151 SOL) | 944 | 51 (19 %) |
| none (any first buy of 0.95 SOL or more by our wallets) | 1,553 | 53 (20 %) |

With no gate at all the rules reach 20 %. 58 more copies are then lost to "one position a coin": the rule fired on
an earlier buy in that coin and the bot bought on a later one. The ceiling with our lists is 151 of 263 (57 %): the
copies with a wallet of ours ahead.

So the recipe is not the bot. It is a rule of ours built from the middle of what the bot does. It fires 15 times
for every time the bot agrees. Whether those other moments pay is the lab's question, not this document's.

### 4. What is not known

- **The bot's wallet list.** 43 % of its copies in 24 hours follow wallets we do not track. `leaders-of` and the
  discovery screen for first-place holders are the tools that close this.
- **The bot's gates.** The bands are quartiles of what it bought. It may have no age or cap gate at all.
- **Why it sells 30 % first on one copy and 20 % or 40 % on another.** The clock is measured; the rule behind it is
  not. One copy in ten sells its first piece in the block it bought in; no rule of ours can.
- **What a 0.03 SOL bid buys at 2 SOL.** The playbook: first behind the wallet in 51 % of blocks, first or second in
  88 %, at bids of 0.015 to 0.04. The lab scores one fixed place a run. So the honest reading is between the lab at
  `+0` (first) and at `+1` (second), never the lab at `+0` alone.
- **The launch half.** Not modelled. The bot buys in the create block behind a creator's own buy of 0.5 SOL or
  more. Our launch buys land a block later, and the lab has no gate for "the creator bought at least this much".
- **The bigger tasks.** Group `big` holds Cupsey's and Gake's wallets. Decu, Slingor and cap are not in it (all
  three are in `wallet`). A rule cannot name one wallet. `6z91-big` and `fhbh1d-big` are the nearest we can run.
- **Copy bots on our own list.** "DecuCopytrade" (7iyYn4, the losing bot) and "Decubot" are labelled `wallet`. In
  the 24 hours no rule fired behind a name that says bot or copy. `--by-wallet` shows who is followed; a copy bot
  there must be taken off the list, since following it is the thing the owner ruled out.
- **Whether any of it pays.** No lab has scored the recipe. The server needs this code first.

### 5. Commands for the server's analyst, after the deploy

Two labs run at a time. All are research: none starts a sniper. They go to the analyst as an idea
(`snipetech remote --wait run operate idea ...`); these are the jobs it starts, as its `start_job` takes them:

```
["lab", "--recipe", "6z91", "--hours", "48", "--copy-position", "+0", "--by-wallet", "--json"]
["lab", "--recipe", "6z91", "--hours", "48", "--copy-position", "+1", "--json"]
["lab", "--recipe", "6z91", "--hours", "48", "--json"]
["lab", "--recipe", "6z91-quick", "--hours", "48", "--copy-position", "+0", "--json"]
["lab", "--recipe", "6z91-big", "--hours", "168", "--copy-position", "+0", "--json"]
["lab", "--recipe", "fhbh1d-big", "--hours", "168", "--copy-position", "+0", "--json"]
["paper", "freeze", "--recipe", "6z91", "--buy-bid", "0", "--hours", "48", "--top", "10"]
```

- The first two are the pair that matters: first behind the wallet (`+0`) and second (`+1`), 2 SOL a buy, the
  0.03 SOL bid charged on every buy. A 0.03 bid is first about half the time and first or second 88 % of the time.
- The third is the same recipe where our copies land today. It still charges the 0.03 bid, so it is the worst case.
- The freeze uses `--buy-bid 0`: the forward test always scores a copy where our copies land today, and our
  measured cost of a transaction already holds the bid that buys that place. It answers "the recipe's moments and
  exit at 2 SOL, at the place we really get".

What a good answer looks like:

- `triggers[].moments` near two days of the table above: about 780 for `wallet`, 670 for `trader`, 80 for `kol`,
  6 for `big` gated, 70 for `big` without gates. Far fewer means a gate or a label group is not reading as meant.
- `recipe.scores[]`: for each rule, `all` and `newer` with `n`, `mean`, `ev_lower`, `win_rate`, `total` for the
  bot's own exit. `strategies[]` is the lab's best exit on the same moments, to compare.
- A finding for the owner is a positive `newer.ev_lower` at `+1`, not only at `+0`, with its landing and bid named.
  It is a yes or no on a size and a bid above today's test limits. It is never a sniper to start: the exit cannot
  be sent.
- A freeze that freezes nothing says why in `skipped` (too few moments, or no positive lower bound).

### 6. How it was measured

Read-only SQL on the server's tape through the signed ops endpoint, and small Python on a local copy. No job was
started on the server and nothing was written to it.

- Every trade of 6Z91 in the 7 days (`trades INDEXED BY trades_user`), each coin's launch row, and every trade of
  the coin in each buy's block and the 4 blocks before it (`trades_mint`).
- A round trip: from a buy with no coins held until 99 % of the coins bought are sold. Seconds are blocks × 0.214.
- A position's worth against its cost: what selling all of it would return at the pool as it stood just before
  the bot's first sell, after the 1.25 % fee, over the SOL of the buy with the fee. These are the units of the
  simulator's stops.
- For each copy, how many buys each wallet ahead of it had made in the coin before that block (one query a copy).
- For the rules' moments: every buy of 0.9 SOL or more by a wallet labelled `wallet` or `kol` in the 24 hours
  (12,675 of 0.95 SOL or more, not by the coin's creator), with the coin's launch time, the reserves after the
  buy, and the wallet's earlier buys in the coin. The funnel: 12,675 buys; 10,020 of at most 3 SOL; 8,987 in a
  coin whose launch is on the tape; 4,984 in a coin 13 s to 7 min old; 2,034 at 47 to 106 SOL; 733 the wallet's
  first buy in the coin; 407 coins.
- Group `big` is 30 wallets, `kol` 18, `wallet` 2,658, `trader` 2,373 of those.

## Lessons

- 6Z91's copy exit is 30 % at 3.2 s, then 17 % every 2.7 s, out by 16 to 20 s (1,638 copies sold in pieces, 7 days); it dropped a copy whole at 2 s on 32 of 1,689 (2 %).
- The "40 % at 2 s, drop at 2 s" clock is its launch snipes (602 of 1,671 dropped whole, −23.7 SOL) and its late buys (211 of 815), not its copies.
- Its drops are a task, not a price rule: a line on price sorts 58 % of 1,635 first sells the way the bot did.
- A typed 1 SOL buy is 0.96 to 0.99 SOL on the tape; a rule that asks for `"sol": 1` passes none of them (155 of the buys 6Z91 followed against 39 at 1.0 to 1.05).
- The playbook's bands (13 s to 7 min, 57 to 122 SOL, 1 to 3 SOL) are 25 % to 75 % marks, and the cap is after the bot's own buy; after the followed buy it is 47 to 106 SOL.
- The recipe covers 29 of the bot's 263 copies in 24 h (11 %); 112 follow wallets we do not track, 110 fail the size, age or cap gate, 9 are lost to one position a coin.
- The recipe fires on 482 moments in 24 h and the bot bought in that block on 33 (7 %): it is a rule of ours, not the bot.
- The bot follows a wallet's first buy in a coin in 88 % of 1,954 copies (94 % behind our wallets): no "any buy" knob is needed.
- 98 % of its copies behind our wallets are behind names the tracker calls traders; group `wallet` also fires behind "Faucet" (72 of 389 moments a day, none taken by the bot).
- An exit in parts would have been sent to OKTech as its plain timer; `oktech.autosell` now refuses it.
