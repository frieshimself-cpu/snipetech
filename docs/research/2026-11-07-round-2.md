## Lessons

- Round 1's creator-claim edge (+0.024, n 48) was hindsight. Claim events come from a scan that keeps only coins that reached 60 SOL by scan time; without hindsight, claims lose -0.0222 (n 162).
- Any screen or lab knob on dev-scan events (fee_claim, dev_transfer, paid_by_dev) must use only what was known at the moment: rows that broke this made +0.088 a trade (n 17, 88 % wins).
- PumpSwap opens at 1.02x the curve's last price, and its first five sells get a median 2.70x within seconds. So a MIG buy behind the 3rd PumpSwap trade comes after the jump; holders at completion capture it.
- MIG-10 on 36 coins: -1.1 % from our buy to +300 s (run 1 +4.6 %, run 2 -5.7 %). Round 1's +6.6 % on 20 coins came from one run.
- Dumps are single trades: a -20 % stop on MIG coins filled near -50 % (once -94 %). A stop cannot cap a bundle dump.
- Copy verdicts catch hot streaks: +0.063 (n 37) in the 6 h before the 14:30 verdicts, -0.0147 (n 257) on the tape after. 16 of 24 verdict wallets never bought again within 9-13 h.
- The copy landing depends on where the leader buys: the model's gap is +18.5 % on curves under 10 % full (n 11) and +2.5 % above (n 246). Live late-curve copies of 7aPo3n landed 0-1 trades behind at +0.5-0.8 % (n 3).
- A close landing is not an edge: mid-curve copies within +2.5 % of the leader still lost -0.016 (n 246), and PumpSwap copies with a +0.2-0.5 % gap lost -0.0214 (n 133).
- Leaders who sell within 25 slots lose most as copies (-0.036, n 64 vs -0.008, n 193). Other buyers crowding in after the leader are not the problem (few -0.042, n 80; many +0.006, n 117).
- Wallet rows from the strategy search can be create-slot artifacts: 7zvP2x had 10 of 10 and 3yrJE1 13 of 20 first buys in the create slot. Drop wallets with 10 %+ of first buys there.
- Size only changes the fixed cost. With 40-53 SOL in the curve, a 1 SOL buy moves the gross by under 1 point, while 0.009 SOL is 4.5/1.8/0.9 % of a 0.2/0.5/1.0 SOL buy. Raise size only when the gross lower bound is above 0.
- Sandwiches are rare on early buys: 0.55-1.1 % in the same slot and 2.9-4.8 % within 2 slots, costing 0.05-0.4 % of a buy on average.
- maxMcapUsd on hunts cuts the better fills. Under 40 SOL in the curve at our landing: -0.0199 (n 745); 50-60 SOL: +0.0043 (n 311). Fills after a 25 %+ run-up lose least (-0.002, n 572).
- Fill exits at the end of their slot, not at their level. In a flood the level fill shows +0.7 % and the end-of-slot fill -8.1 % on the same 205 moments; outside floods the two agree within about 1 point.
