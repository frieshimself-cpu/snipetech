## Lessons

- The lab, paper and autotest score LaunchLab coins that pump.fun snipers cannot buy: 30-60 % of creator-0 firings on 7-8 Nov, and all of bbe92cca81's paper profit (+0.095 on 3 LaunchLab rows; pump.fun -0.048 on 4).
- Check a lab's venue mix before believing a creator-0 test part; this round's creator-0 labs (+0.0144 on 13, +0.0215 on 13) probably include LaunchLab rows.
- Wallet identity beats shape: the same 15 wallets sit in all 27 mode A coins and in no other create slot, and the census found a second play (mode B, 44 coins) that shape never saw.
- On operator coins the simulator is 0.02-0.04 a trade kinder than what real +3@1 neighbours took home (real median +0.005, mean -0.007, on 29 wallets).
- The calm-hour crowd hunt is refuted: 138 calm fills at -1.98 % gross against a +4.5 % bar.
- Recurring bundle crews other than the 41 SOL operator lose at our landing: -0.0305 on 260 discovery coins, -0.0232 on 217 test coins; crews rotate wallets within about a day.
- LaunchLab slots are 0.217 s, not 0.4 s: q8's '60 s' timers were about 33 s.
- LaunchLab 'does not rug in 20 s' is true only of the deep config (about 228 SOL virtual quote); shallow configs lost -0.0257 on 50 with 5 rugs.
- Every-launch LaunchLab loses (test -0.0104 on 482); its median of about -0.015 is the round-trip floor of about 2 % fee plus 0.0084 SOL.
- Wallets that sold at 11-30 s made about +0.012 median at our landing, but a fixed 20 s or 60 s timer on the same entries loses: their edge is in choosing when to sell.
- Market regimes here last hours: the LaunchLab wave fell from 189 to 13 launches an hour in 7 h; the operator has been silent 16 h; the fee389 operator's first burst lost -0.104 on all 6 coins.
- A lab picks its own exit on train: deep35's lab chose a 60 s trail, not the declared 15 s timer, so its number does not test the hypothesis's exit.
